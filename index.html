01 — The film

Press play. Then pack.

Culebra is small enough to see in a day and easy enough to get wrong. Most people spend their first three hours figuring out what a local knows before breakfast — the ferry line, the golf-cart rules, which side of Flamenco has shade at noon.

This guide is the shortcut. No 40-minute vlog, no drone footage set to music. Just the route, the timing and the addresses, in the order you'll actually need them.

04 — Questions

The ones we get most

Can Culebra be done as a day trip?

Yes, and the film is built around that version of the day.

Length of the ferry ride

From 50 minutes to an hour and 10 minutes. The ride to Culebra can be choppy depending on the sea, but the ride back tends to be smoother.

Do I need to rent a golf cart?

Not for Flamenco alone. For anything past it, yes.

How do I get the video?

It streams right here on this page — nothing to install, nothing to download. Bookmark it and open it again when you're on the island.
